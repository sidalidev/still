# Privacy

Still collects nothing.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. This app has no internet access.

- **Your sessions.** They stay in the app's private storage. They leave the app only if you export them yourself, as a file saved where you choose.
- **Backups.** Still's data is excluded from cloud backups and phone-to-phone transfers.
- **Links.** Links in About open in your browser or mail app, only when you tap them. Still itself never connects to anything.

The same list is in the app, under About, "What leaves your device".

## What stays on the phone

- Your sessions: when each one started, its mode (Calm, Sleep or Trust) and its length in minutes. This is how Still shows the minutes you breathed today.
- Your settings: sound, haptics, ambience and the length of each mode.

Nothing else. No name, no account, no identifier, no location, no health data.

## Export and erase

In Settings:

- **Export my sessions** opens the system file picker and saves them as `still-sessions.json`, wherever you choose (a folder on the phone, a USB drive, a cloud app you picked). Still writes that one file and nothing else. It never shares the file by itself.
- **Erase everything** deletes your sessions and settings from the phone at once. You type ERASE to confirm, because it cannot be undone.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything too.
