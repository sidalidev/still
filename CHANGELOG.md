# Changelog

## 0.1.0

**New**

- Three ways to breathe: Calm (in 5, out 5, for 3, 5 or 10 minutes), Sleep (slows down to 4-7-8 on a black screen and stops by itself) and Trust (one verse of the Quran, then 3 minutes of Calm).
- An orb that breathes with you, haptics that rise and fall with each breath, soft tones and an optional ambience: wind, rain or night.
- The minutes you breathed today at the end of each session. Export your sessions or erase everything from Settings.

**Improved**

- Nothing yet: this is the first release.

**Known issues**

- The signing certificate fingerprint is published with this release, not before.
