# Interruptions

Every notification, dialog, banner, badge and counter Still can show, why it is there, and how to turn it off. Still sends no notification to bring you back. Seven days without opening the app means zero notifications.

| What | When | Who asked for it | How to turn it off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Never. Still has no notification permission. | Nobody | Nothing to turn off |
| App icon badge | Never | Nobody | Nothing to turn off |
| Confirmation before Erase everything (type ERASE) | When you tap Erase everything, because it cannot be undone | You, by tapping Erase everything | Cancel it |
| End of session screen | When a session ends, or when you stop it | You, by starting a session | Tap Done, or leave the app |
| Sounds | During a session, only when the phone is not on silent or vibrate | The sound setting, on by default | The sound setting, or silent mode |
| Haptics | During a session, with each breath | The haptics setting, on by default | The haptics setting, or the system haptics setting |

Rule: anything new here needs a trigger the user started, and a way to turn it off.
