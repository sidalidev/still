<b>Breathe slowly. Calm down, or fall asleep.</b>

Pick a mode, tap Begin, and follow the orb: it grows as you breathe in, rests when you hold, and shrinks as you breathe out. Soft tones and a gentle vibration keep you in time, even with your eyes closed.

<b>Three modes</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Calm</b>: in for 5 seconds, out for 5, six breaths a minute, for 3, 5 or 10 minutes.</li>
<li><b>Sleep</b>: starts at in 4, out 6, and slows down to 4-7-8 on a black screen. Stops by itself after 10, 15 or 20 minutes.</li>
<li><b>Trust</b>: one verse of the Quran about trusting God, to read calmly, then 3 minutes of Calm. Arabic and Pickthall's English translation, no hadith, no commentary.</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>No internet permission: the app cannot connect to anything.</li>
<li>Only your sessions are stored (date, mode, minutes), on your phone. Export them, or erase everything at once.</li>
<li>One permission: vibration.</li>
<li>No account, no ads, no analytics, no streaks, no reminders.</li>
</ul>

Sounds play only when your phone is not on silent or vibrate. Haptics follow your system vibration settings. Both can be turned off in Settings.
