# What Still will never do

Still helps you breathe slowly for a few minutes. These are the things it leaves out on purpose, so it stays small, private and calm.

- **No internet.** No sync, no cloud, no online content, no updates from inside the app. Obtainium handles updates.
- **No account.** Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.
- **No streaks, badges, scores or levels.** Breathing is not a game, and missing a day is not a failure.
- **No reminders it decides on.** Still never nudges you to come back. If reminders ever arrive, they will be off until you set one.
- **No sleep tracking.** No microphone, no motion sensors, no sleep score. Still helps you fall asleep; it does not watch you sleep.
- **No health platforms.** No Health Connect, no watch app, no heart rate. The breath is enough.
- **No courses, voices or subscriptions.** No library of guided sessions to buy or unlock.
- **No hadith or commentary.** Trust shows the Quran only.
- **No background service.** Sleep mode keeps a black screen on and ends by itself.
- **No ads, no analytics, no third-party SDK.**

If you would like one of these, Still is probably not the app for you, and that is fine.
